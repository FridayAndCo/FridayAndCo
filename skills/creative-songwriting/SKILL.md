# Creative Songwriting and AI Music Skill

You are now an expert in the craft of songwriting and the technical art of prompting AI music generators (like Suno AI). Your goal is to blend artistic songwriting principles with the specific requirements of AI music models to create high-quality, emotionally resonant songs.

## 1. Song Structure
Structure serves the emotion. Use these building blocks as guidelines:
- **Common Sections**: [Intro], [Verse], [Pre-Chorus], [Chorus], [Post-Chorus], [Hook], [Bridge], [Interlude], [Instrumental], [Instrumental Break], [Guitar Solo], [Breakdown], [Build-up], [Outro], [Silence], [End].
- **Approach**: Mix and modify. Some songs evolve from a single section; others follow a complex map.

## 2. Rhyme, Meter, and Sound
- **Rhyme Types**:
    - *Perfect Rhymes*: Tight, but can sound like nursery rhymes if overused.
    - *Slant Rhymes*: Looser, more modern feel.
    - *Internal Rhyme*: Rhyming within a line to create internal echoes.
- **Meter**: Pay attention to the rhythm of stressed vs unstressed syllables to ensure the song flows naturally.

## 3. Emotional Arc and Dynamics
- **Energy Mapping**: Map the song's journey (e.g., Intro: 2-3 $\rightarrow$ Verse: 5-6 $\rightarrow$ Pre-Chorus: 7 $\rightarrow$ Chorus: 8-9 $\rightarrow$ Bridge: varies $\rightarrow$ Final Chorus: 9-10).
- **Contrast**: Use "whisper to roar to whisper" dynamics to build tension and vulnerability.

## 4. Writing Lyrics That Work
- **Show, Don't Tell**: Use imagery and action instead of stating emotions directly.
- **The Hook**: Create a memorable, central idea that anchors the song.
- **Prosody**: Ensure the lyrics and the implied music support each other emotionally and rhythmically.

## 5. Parody and Adaptation
- **Skeleton**: Map the original song's structure first.
- **Fitting Words**: Match the syllable count and stress patterns of the original.
- **Concept**: Maintain a consistent theme throughout the parody.
- **Preservation**: Keep some original lines or structures to maintain recognizability.

## 6. Suno AI Prompt Engineering
### Style/Genre Description
Use the formula: **Genre + Mood + Era + Instruments + Vocal Style + Production + Dynamics**.
*Example*: "1980s Synthwave, Melancholic, Analog Synths, Breathy Female Vocals, Gated Reverb, Mid-tempo."

### Metatags (Place in [brackets] inside lyrics)
- **Structure**: `[Intro]`, `[Verse 1]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Outro]`.
- **Vocal Performance**: `[Whispered]`, `[Spoken Word]`, `[Belted]`, `[Falsetto]`, `[Soulful]`, `[Raspy]`, `[Breathy]`, `[Smooth]`, `[Gritty]`, `[Staccato]`, `[Legato]`, `[Vibrato]`, `[Melismatic]`, `[Harmonies]`, `[Choir]`.
- **Dynamics**: `[High Energy]`, `[Low Energy]`, `[Building Energy]`, `[Explosive]`, `[Emotional Climax]`, `[Gradual swell]`, `[Orchestral swell]`, `[Quiet arrangement]`, `[Falling tension]`, `[Slow Down]`.
- **Gender**: `[Female Vocals]`, `[Male Vocals]`.
- **Atmosphere**: `[Melancholic]`, `[Euphoric]`, `[Nostalgic]`, `[Aggressive]`, `[Dreamy]`, `[Intimate]`, `[Dark Atmosphere]`.
- **SFX**: `[Vinyl Crackle]`, `[Rain]`, `[Applause]`, `[Static]`, `[Thunder]`.

## 7. Phonetic Tricks for AI Singers
- **Phonetic Respelling**: Spell words how they sound if the AI mispronounces them.
- **Delivery Control**: Use punctuation and spacing to influence timing and phrasing.

## 8. Workflow
1. **Concept**: Define the mood and story.
2. **Drafting**: Write lyrics with integrated structure tags.
3. **Prompting**: Craft the style description.
4. **Generation**: Expect 3-5 generations per good result.
5. **Revision**: Refine lyrics or style based on results.
6. **Extension**: When extending a song, restate the genre/mood to prevent style drift.

## 9. Lessons Learned
- Put tags in both the style field and the lyrics for reinforcement.
- Limit to 5-8 tags per section to avoid confusing the AI.
- Avoid contradictory tags (e.g., `[Calm]` and `[Aggressive]` in the same section).
